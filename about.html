About The Game

BGMI is a fast-paced battle royale game where 100 players drop into a battlefield and fight for survival. Players must loot weapons, use strategy, and survive till the last moment to achieve victory.

It is one of the most competitive mobile esports games in India, requiring teamwork, reflex, and smart gameplay decisions.

Every match is a test of skill, survival instinct, and teamwork under pressure.
